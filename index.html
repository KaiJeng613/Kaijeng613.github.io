My Career Journey

Over the past seven years, my work as a software engineer has spanned enterprise web platforms, financial integrations and self-service systems. These experiences have broadened my focus from individual features to how applications, data and operational workflows fit together.

Building My Foundation

My earlier project work included digital experiences for Resorts World Genting and the migration of its legacy CMS to Adobe Experience Cloud. Working across web applications and content workflows helped me understand how engineering decisions support both customers and the teams managing a platform.

Connecting Systems & Workflows

My experience also grew to include payment integrations and AML/KYC automation. Building backend middleware for GoPayz gave me experience connecting services, while work on compliance workflows strengthened my attention to data handling and transactional reliability.

Learning from Real Operations

Working with biometric enrollment and hotel self-service kiosks added another perspective: software needs to work alongside physical devices and everyday operations. These projects shaped my preference for practical solutions, clear logging and systems that other engineers can understand and maintain.

Continuing into Blockchain & AI

Today, I work at Etherscan and continue to explore blockchain, AI and developer tools. Alongside my professional work, side projects and ongoing learning give me room to experiment and develop new skills.
